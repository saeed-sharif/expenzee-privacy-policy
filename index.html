Privacy Policy

App Title: Expenzee: Track & Split

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information in the Expenzee app (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your financial data remains secure.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect the following information:

  • Local Data: If you use the App without signing in, all financial data (expenses, income, wallets) is stored locally on your device.
  • Authentication Data: If you sign in with Google, we collect your Name and Email Address to provide cloud backup and group sharing features.
  • Group Data: Information provided during group creation (Member Names, Phone Numbers, and Transaction Details) is stored to facilitate bill splitting.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the features of the App, including:

  • Managing personal and group expenses.
  • Real-time syncing of group transactions between members.
  • Enabling account recovery and cloud backup for registered users.
  • Generating financial insights and spending summaries.

3. Data Storage and Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Local Storage: Encrypted on-device storage for all users.
  • Cloud Sync: For signed-in users, data is securely synced and stored using Google Firebase/Firestore servers.
  • We do not sell or share your personal financial records with any third-party advertisers.

4. Account and Data Deletion

You have full control over your data.

  • Manual Deletion: You can delete specific transactions or groups at any time within the app.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: Registered users can use the "Delete Account" feature in the Profile section. This will permanently remove your profile and all your synced data from our cloud servers.

5. Permissions

The App requires Internet Access for cloud syncing and authentication features. We do not access your location, contacts, or camera without explicit additional permission.

6. Third-Party Services

We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore (by Google) to provide secure login and real-time database services. These services handle data according to Google's professional privacy standards.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our features or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available within the App.
