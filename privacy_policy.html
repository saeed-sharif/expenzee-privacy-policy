Privacy Policy

App Title: Expenzee: Track & Split

Last updated: August 08, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information in the Expenzee app (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your financial data remains secure and private.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal or financial information. All your data, including income, expenses, wallet details, and group transactions, is stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the core features of the App, including:

  • Adding and managing income, expenses, and wallets.
  • Category-based organization of your personal finances.
  • Group bill splitting and debt tracking.
  • Automated financial summaries and spending insights generated locally on your device based on your entries.

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored securely in your device's local database. We do not use any external cloud servers to sync or backup your data. This means:

  • We do not have access to your financial records.
  • Your data is never shared with third parties.
  • If you uninstall the App or clear its storage data, your records will be permanently removed unless you have a separate system backup.

4. Permissions

The App is designed to operate with minimal permissions. We do not access your contacts, location, camera, or any other sensitive device data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any of your data to third parties. Your data stays entirely on your mobile device.

6. Automated Insights & Summaries

The financial summaries and category reports provided in the App are processed locally on your device using built-in algorithms. No data is processed externally or transmitted to remote servers.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected directly on this page.
